2026

AI Accelerator RTL Design Intern

KLE TECHNOLOGICAL UNIVERSITY · TECHXCELERATE 2026 · BELAGAVI

  • Designed a 4x4 weight-stationary systolic array (signed INT8 inputs, 32-bit accumulation) in SystemVerilog with parameterized modules for the PE, PE array, weight loader, activation skew, output collector, and control FSM.
  • Pipelined multiply-accumulate datapath with diagonal activation skew and FSM-controlled preload, compute, and drain phases; one 4x4 multiply completes in 18 clock cycles (1.5 µs at 12 MHz).
  • Implemented on iCE40UP5K (VSDSquadron FPGA Mini) with Yosys and nextpnr at 12 MHz, with a UART interface, power-on reset, and cycle counter; 16 multipliers mapped to LUTs since the array exceeds the 8 DSP blocks.
2026

RISC-V SoC and FPGA IP Development Intern

VLSI SYSTEM DESIGN (VSD) · VIRTUAL

  • Designed and integrated 3 memory-mapped peripheral IPs (GPIO, Multi-Register GPIO, PWM) into a custom 32-bit RISC-V SoC in Verilog RTL, with 8+ control, status, and data registers and SoC-level address decoding.
  • Achieved timing closure at 20.34 MHz across CPU, memory, UART, GPIO, and PWM subsystems; 1,638 logic cells (31% utilization) and 16 BRAM blocks on iCE40UP5K.
